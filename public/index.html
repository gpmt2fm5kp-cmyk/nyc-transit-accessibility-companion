Access Granted is a solo class project by Melissa Noel. It explains reference information for 15 selected NYC subway and Staten Island Railway station locations and complexes.

What the cards tell you

MTA Open Data provides station, service, and direction-specific accessibility flags. Entrance descriptions come from MTA’s accessible-station reference. Every card links to its sources and shows the actual date checked.

What they cannot confirm

We fetch MTA elevator and escalator outage reports when you choose a station, refresh the status, and every five minutes while the page is visible. Reports may be delayed and do not guarantee an entire step-free journey. We do not check service changes, closures, or opening hours. Check the official MTA source before traveling. Missing information means unknown.

Snapshots and sample outages

Reference records are bundled with this version. Station reference refresh is not automatic. Elevator reports are checked separately using MTA’s equipment and outage feeds (cached up to one minute). The sample-outage switch shows an invented scenario and never changes the underlying station record.

Your information

Station questions are sent to Google Gemini when the AI guide is connected. Conversation history stays in page memory and is cleared when you change stations or reload. The app does not deliberately store or log questions; hosting and AI providers may process request information under their own policies. Only your appearance preference is saved on this device.
